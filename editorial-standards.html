Trust and accountability

Editorial Standards

How GAL researches, reviews, discloses relationships, and maintains independent equipment coverage.

Editorial independence

GAL’s editorial decisions are based on reader usefulness, research value, and relevance to amateur golfers. Affiliate commissions, product samples, advertising, or retailer relationships do not determine conclusions.

Evidence and attribution

We distinguish manufacturer specifications, independent measurements, published research, hands-on observations, and editorial opinions. Important technical claims should be sourced or clearly labeled as analysis.

Reviews and comparisons

Every product has strengths, limitations, and an intended golfer. We avoid declaring universal winners when golfer needs differ.

Product samples

When equipment is supplied by a manufacturer or retailer, that relationship will be disclosed. Providing a sample does not guarantee coverage or a favorable conclusion.

Affiliate relationships

Affiliate links are a secondary purchasing convenience. They are disclosed near relevant links and do not influence rankings.

Artificial intelligence

GAL may use software tools, including AI-assisted tools, for organization, drafting support, transcription, or analysis. Published material remains subject to human editorial review, verification, and accountability.

Updates and corrections

Articles may be updated when models, prices, rules, specifications, or evidence changes. Material factual corrections are noted in accordance with our Corrections Policy.
