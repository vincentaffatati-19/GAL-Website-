How the tool calculates

The cost equations

The calculator separates the economic value of balls consumed from the cash required to purchase whole packages.

Price per ball = Package price ÷ Package quantity Balls consumed = Rounds × (Lost + Retired) Economic cost = Price per ball × Balls consumed Packages = ceil(max(0, Consumed − Starting inventory) ÷ Package quantity) Cash outlay = Packages × Package price + Tax + Shipping
