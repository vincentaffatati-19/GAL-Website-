The Four C’s

Construction

Golf-ball layers are engineered to create different responses from the driver, irons, wedges, and putter.

Learning Center / The Four C’s / Construction

Two-piece construction

A large core and durable cover commonly emphasize speed, durability, and value. The category includes many distance-oriented and recreational products.

Three-piece and multilayer construction

Additional layers allow designers to tune how the ball responds at different impact speeds. More layers do not automatically make a ball better, but they may provide more design flexibility.

Why construction matters

Construction influences the relationship among speed, launch, spin, feel, and short-game response. The cover and internal layers must be evaluated together.

GAL takeaway: Layer count is a design description, not a quality score.
