How Golf Analytics Lab uses affiliate links while keeping editorial decisions independent.

How affiliate links work

Some links on Golf Analytics Lab are affiliate links. When a reader clicks an affiliate link and completes an eligible purchase, GAL may receive a commission. The reader does not pay an additional amount because of the affiliate relationship.

Our editorial promise

Affiliate relationships do not determine which products we cover, how products are ranked, or what conclusions we publish. We may recommend a product that is not available through an affiliate partner, and we may advise readers not to buy a product when it does not fit their needs.

Link-level disclosures

Pages containing affiliate links will include a clear disclosure near the relevant recommendation or purchasing section. Examples include “paid link,” “commissions earned,” or a plain-language statement that GAL may earn a commission.

Pricing and availability

Prices and availability can change. Unless live data is supplied through an approved retailer tool, GAL avoids presenting a changing retailer price as guaranteed or current.

Other relationships

Product samples, sponsored content, travel support, or other material relationships will be disclosed separately when applicable. Sponsored content will be identified as sponsored and will not be presented as independent editorial coverage.
