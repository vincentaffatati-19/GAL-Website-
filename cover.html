The Four C’s

Cover

The cover is the golfer’s direct contact point with every club and a major influence on durability, feel, spin, and price.

Learning Center / The Four C’s / Cover

Ionomer-family covers

Ionomer covers are typically durable and cost-efficient. They are widely used in value, distance, and recreational golf balls.

Urethane covers

Urethane covers are commonly used in tour-style and premium balls because they can support softer feel and greater short-game interaction, though they usually cost more and may show wear sooner.

Cover is not the whole ball

A urethane ball is not automatically the right choice for every golfer, and an ionomer ball is not automatically low performance. Construction, aerodynamics, consistency, and golfer needs remain important.

GAL takeaway: Choose cover material by the performance tradeoff you value—not by prestige alone.
