The Four C’s

Cost

Golf-ball price reflects materials, manufacturing, consistency, distribution, marketing, and brand positioning—but more expensive does not always mean more useful.

Learning Center / The Four C’s / Cost

Value tier

Value products often prioritize durability, straightforward construction, and lower replacement cost. This can be highly practical for golfers who lose several balls per round.

Mid-price tier

Mid-tier products may blend softer feel, multilayer construction, or more specialized performance while staying below flagship pricing.

Premium tier

Premium balls commonly use urethane covers, multilayer construction, tighter market positioning, extensive R&D, and tour validation. The benefits matter most when they align with the golfer’s needs.

Cost per round

The most useful value calculation is not price per dozen alone. Consider lost-ball rate, durability, consistency, and whether the performance difference is noticeable in your game.

GAL takeaway: The best value ball is the least expensive ball that reliably delivers the performance you need.
