About GAL

Independent analysis for everyday golfers

Golf Analytics Lab turns equipment specifications, research, and testing into practical guidance.

Our mission

Golf Analytics Lab helps amateur golfers understand equipment and make informed choices without relying solely on manufacturer marketing, tour usage, or price.

What makes GAL different

We organize equipment information around practical decisions. For golf balls, that begins with the Four C’s: Compression, Construction, Cover, and Cost. For clubs, it includes fit, forgiveness, launch, consistency, and value.

Independent by design

Editorial conclusions are developed separately from affiliate relationships. Products are not ranked higher because they offer a commission, and availability at a specific retailer is not treated as proof that a product is better.

Who we serve

GAL is built primarily for recreational and amateur golfers who want straightforward explanations, useful comparisons, and realistic recommendations.
