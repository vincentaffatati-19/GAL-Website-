Build a Better Bag

We examine every piece of golf equipment from the point of creation to the point of purchase—then turn the full story into practical reviews, comparisons, and buyers guides.

Every club has a story. Every purchase has a tradeoff.

Build a Better Bag follows golf equipment from its earliest purpose and first innovators through modern engineering, manufacturing, pricing, branding, regulation, and retail. Each installment concludes with practical guidance designed to help golfers choose equipment that fits their game, preferences, and budget.

Where innovation meets the rules

Golf-equipment companies are free to innovate only within boundaries established by the USGA and The R&A. Build a Better Bag explains those boundaries, how equipment is tested for conformity, and how regulation influences design, marketing, performance, and product strategy.

Depending on the category, coverage may include clubhead volume, spring-like effect, characteristic time, moment of inertia, groove dimensions, golf-ball weight and diameter, overall distance standards, putter form, anchoring rules, rangefinder restrictions, and conforming equipment lists.
