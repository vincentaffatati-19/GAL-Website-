Our team

Meet the people behind GAL

Golf Analytics Lab brings together expertise in research, precision measurement, law, finance, marketing, social media, technology, and golf-media production to create independent, practical equipment guidance for everyday golfers.

One operating system

Different disciplines. One clear mission.

GAL combines research, measurement, business analysis, legal review, digital communication, and golf-media experience. Each discipline strengthens the same goal: helping golfers understand equipment and make better decisions.

How the lab works

Evidence moves through the team

Every guide and tool passes through a repeatable sequence before it reaches the golfer.

  1. 01ResearchFind the claims, sources, history, and market context.
  2. 02MeasureTest methods, data quality, consistency, and practical significance.
  3. 03InterpretTurn technical findings into useful equipment guidance.
  4. 04Protect & publishReview accuracy, risk, clarity, and communication before release.
