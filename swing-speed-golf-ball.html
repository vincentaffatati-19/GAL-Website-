Does Swing Speed Determine Your Golf Ball?

Swing speed can inform golf-ball selection, but it should not become the entire fitting process.

Learning Center / Golfer Education

Swing speed is context—not a complete fitting

Swing speed helps describe impact conditions, but golfers with similar speed may deliver the club differently and need different flight, spin, feel, and short-game performance.

What else matters

Consider driver launch and spin, iron trajectory, wedge control, feel preference, weather, typical course conditions, and how often you lose a ball.

A better process

Use swing speed to narrow the market, then compare a small number of balls from green to tee. Begin with putting and wedges, move to irons, and finish with the driver.

GAL takeaway: Fit the whole game, not one number.
