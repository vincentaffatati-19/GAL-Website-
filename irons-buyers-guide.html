HOW V3.1 DIFFERS

From category scoring to distance-from-ideal scoring

01

Strike Fit

Industry category supplies the starting point, but offset and GAL architecture signals refine the score when enough public data exists.

02

Price Fit

Price is continuous. A $115 iron and a $145 iron no longer receive the exact same value treatment simply because both are under $150.

03

Loft & Shape Fit

Published 7-iron loft and offset are compared with an ideal target derived from the golfer's flight and address preferences.

04

Different Jobs

The shortlist identifies Best Overall, Best Value, More Forgiving, More Compact, Distance Alternative, or Premium Alternative where applicable.

GAL methodology: Fit is an equipment-decision aid built from public specifications and GAL v1.6 architecture data. It is not measured launch-monitor performance. Value is calculated only when a published per-iron price exists. EGI and FAS-H remain engineering/specification signals, not measured performance or forgiveness.

BEFORE YOU BUY

Three checks GAL would make next

01

Can you launch it?

Compare peak height and descent angle—not just carry distance.

02

What happens when you miss?

Compare toe, heel, thin, and low-face strikes. Watch ball-speed retention, carry loss, and dispersion.

03

Does the whole set gap?

A great 7-iron does not guarantee a great set. Check useful carry gaps from the longest iron through the wedges.
