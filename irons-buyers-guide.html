BEFORE YOU BUY

Three checks GAL would make next

01

Can you launch it?

Compare peak height and descent angle—not just carry distance. Long irons that fly too low are difficult to hold on greens.

02

What happens when you miss?

Compare toe, heel, thin, and low-face strikes. Watch ball-speed retention, carry loss, and dispersion.

03

Does the whole set gap?

A great 7-iron does not guarantee a great set. Check useful carry gaps from the longest iron through the wedges.

GAL methodology: v2.2 uses a weighted fit model: Strike consistency 35%, Price 30%, Goal 15%, Look 10%, Flight 8%, and Data Confidence 2%. Rankings use published specifications and market positioning from GAL Irons Database v1.6. EGI and FAS-H remain engineering/specification signals, not measured performance scores.
