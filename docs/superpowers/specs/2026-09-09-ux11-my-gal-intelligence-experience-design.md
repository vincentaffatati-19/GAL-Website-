# GAL UX11 — My GAL Intelligence Experience Design

**Version:** UX11.0  
**Initial review candidate:** GAL-UX11.0-RC1  
**Status:** DESIGN LOCK / PRE-IMPLEMENTATION  
**Date:** 2026-09-09

## Purpose

UX11 establishes a clean, governed continuation of the approved My GAL experience after the UX10.03 Better Bag Scorecard experiment was determined to be the wrong product direction.

UX11 is not a redesign from scratch. It combines the strongest proven components from the approved UX10 lineage:

- the refined photographic My GAL presentation;
- the governed UX10.02 Golfer Profile and dynamic golfer visual system;
- the corrected My Bag → Golfer Profile → Recommendations flow;
- current governed equipment imagery and category recommendation engines;
- mandatory artifact provenance and visual-regression gates.

## Product identity

**Workstream name:** `GAL UX11 — My GAL Intelligence Experience`

The logged-in GAL experience remains profile-centered. The golfer’s governed longitudinal profile drives equipment insight and recommendations. Recommendations are a consequence of the profile and equipment evidence; they are not a competing cross-category scoring dashboard.

## Primary navigation

The five primary My GAL destinations remain:

1. Today
2. My Bag
3. Insights
4. Guides
5. Progress

`Golfer Profile` remains a separate destination/control and is not promoted into the five primary navigation items.

## My GAL visual direction

The approved visual target is the refined photographic My GAL dashboard represented by the recovered `My Gal Dashboard with landing.png` reference.

Required presentation characteristics:

- photographic coastal/famous-course tee-box environment;
- realistic approved GAL bag treatment;
- governed club/product imagery;
- equipment-category callouts around the bag;
- Bag Status;
- Next Opportunity;
- Bag Value;
- Bag User’s Guide;
- Quick Actions;
- Recent Insight;
- Progress at a Glance;
- contextual equipment intelligence drawer/panel;
- official Option 7A Motion Arch visual identity and GAL Navy/Orange system.

The tee-box scene is a presentation layer. The bag visual is a separate presentation layer. Changing either must not silently change governed analytical state.

### Explicit visual prohibition

Do not replace approved photographic course/bag/product visuals with CSS-drawn scenery, gradients pretending to be scenery, generic silhouettes, or regenerated approximations when approved assets can be recovered.

## Golfer Profile foundation

The authoritative profile implementation source is `GAL-UX10.02-FINAL-PROFILE-RC.html` plus `GAL-UX10.02-FINAL-PROFILE-ASSET-MANIFEST.json` and its QA/static verification.

UX11 must preserve:

- Controlled Body / Game / Swing fields;
- governed longitudinal profile behavior and provenance;
- unknown facts remain unknown;
- Female and Male remain authoritative when age is undisclosed;
- Junior / 18–34 / 35–54 / 55–64 / 65+ routing;
- Neutral Junior and Neutral Adult fallbacks;
- right-handed base with left-handed photographic presentation mirror;
- native-aspect four-stage Swing Breakdown: Address, Backswing, Impact, Finish;
- collapsed `+ / −` disclosure pattern for known profile sections;
- Your Miss graphic isolated from Save controls;
- current profile revision exposed to downstream recommendation consumers.

The final governed visual system contains 12 Body masters and 12 Swing plates. A visual-library poster is reference material only and must not replace the manifest-defined asset system.

## My Bag and equipment intelligence

My Bag remains the golfer’s equipment-centered scene, not a generic profile dashboard.

Required behavior:

- show current bag categories and club state without fabricating unsupported status;
- preserve 14-club semantics; golf ball does not count as a club;
- warn on duplicate clubs when appropriate;
- use actual governed bag contents when available;
- retain truthful incomplete/unknown states when evidence is insufficient;
- use governed product imagery from the current equipment-image pipelines;
- no promotional badges, Hot List/award graphics, retailer watermarks, guessed fallback images, or unsupported backgrounds;
- if a governed product image is unavailable, show an explicit hold/missing state rather than invent imagery.

## Recommendations

`Top 3` means the **top three product recommendations inside an equipment/category context** such as Driver, Golf Ball, Irons, Wedges, Fairway Woods/Hybrids, or Putter.

UX11 must not restore the rejected UX10.03 interpretation of `Top 3` as the golfer’s top three equipment categories or as a cross-category Better Bag priority ranking.

Recommendation rules:

- generated by the relevant existing governed Buyers Guide/category engine;
- based on the active governed golfer profile revision plus relevant equipment/performance evidence;
- each recommendation retains fit reasoning and source/provenance where the engine provides it;
- insufficient data must remain visible as insufficient rather than producing fabricated scores;
- compare and canonical Add to Bag behavior remain available where supported;
- user and customer/subscriber portal implications must be updated together.

## Rejected / superseded branch

`UX10.03 Better Bag Scorecard` is explicitly **REJECTED / SUPERSEDED** as a baseline for UX11.

Do not carry forward as primary hierarchy:

- Better Bag Priority Index;
- cross-category “Your Top 3 Better Bag Priorities”;
- invented whole-bag/category scores;
- a new standalone dashboard that competes with the profile-centered My GAL architecture.

UX10.03 artifacts may be retained only as historical/reference material.

## Artifact provenance requirements

UX11 is governed by `docs/GAL_ARTIFACT_PROVENANCE_AND_RC_ASSEMBLY_RULE.md`.

Before RC implementation, the assembly manifest must identify separately:

- architecture source;
- visual source;
- implementation source;
- asset manifest;
- supersession lineage;
- QA evidence;
- explicit inclusions/exclusions;
- open release blockers.

No source may be selected solely because its filename says `final`, `complete`, has a higher numeric suffix, or has the latest timestamp.

## Visual regression requirements

A UX11 RC does not pass merely because HTML/JS tests pass.

At minimum, QA must compare rendered UX11 surfaces against the approved reference for:

- photographic scene quality;
- approved GAL bag appearance;
- product image quality;
- official brand treatment;
- panel hierarchy and layout;
- golfer visual family and swing proportions;
- desktop and mobile behavior;
- absence of crude CSS stand-ins or superseded visual assets.

If browser automation is blocked by environment policy, that must be reported as blocked. A static test may not be represented as a browser visual pass.

## Data and governance constraints

- Unknown facts stay unknown.
- No fabricated golfer metrics, product rankings, bag values, or opportunities.
- User override precedence and provenance/history remain governed.
- Location/environment facts retire correctly when location changes.
- External platform integrations remain outstanding and the overall implementation is not considered fully complete until those integrations are completed and verified.
- Production golfer data is not copied into non-production environments unless explicitly anonymized/sanitized.
