Corrections Policy

How readers can report errors and how GAL documents material corrections.

Our commitment

GAL aims to correct meaningful factual errors promptly and transparently.

How to request a correction

Send the article title, URL, disputed statement, supporting source, and your contact information to corrections@golfanalyticslab.com.

What happens next

The editorial team reviews the request, checks relevant sources, and determines whether a correction, clarification, update, or no change is appropriate.

Correction notes

Material corrections will generally be noted at the bottom of the affected article with the date and a concise explanation. Minor spelling, formatting, or style edits may be corrected without a formal note when they do not alter meaning.

Updates versus corrections

An update reflects new information or a changed product market. A correction addresses information that was inaccurate when published.
