Methods

How we test and evaluate golf equipment

GAL separates hands-on testing, controlled measurement, specification analysis, and editorial judgment.

Our content labels

Lab Tested

Measurements or controlled procedures conducted by GAL using documented equipment and conditions.

Course Tested

Hands-on use during golf practice or play. Course testing provides practical observations but may not isolate every performance variable.

Specification Analysis

Analysis based on manufacturer specifications, technical documents, rules, patents, and other published sources. This label does not imply hands-on testing.

Retail Comparison

A structured comparison of available products, pricing tiers, construction, and positioning at one or more retailers.

First Look

An early overview based on available information. It is not a final review.

Measurement principles

GAL aims to document the equipment used, test conditions, sample size, limitations, and whether results are measured or observed. Repeatability and transparency are more important than creating false precision.

Golf-ball framework

Golf-ball analysis begins with Compression, Construction, Cover, and Cost, then considers flight, spin, feel, durability, consistency, and golfer fit when evidence is available.

Important: Individual results vary with swing delivery, club fit, weather, course conditions, and player preference. GAL testing is educational and does not guarantee performance.
